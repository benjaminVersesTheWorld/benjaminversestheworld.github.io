As a domesticated man, the fish mac is really unbeatable. The prospect of that cheesy, sticky, fried goodness preoccupies the mind as all other aspects of life take a backseat when the destination is as smooth and even half as crunchy . I want to shout it from the roof tops when there is fish mac on the way. I feel cheated when I am denied the chance to lord the eminence of excellence that is soon to visit me; not satisfied with gloating once only after the fact. I do not think it cruel but aspirational that my peers may rise from their wallows and angst to know and feast upon one of the most delicious meals known to mam. Fish-mac.
